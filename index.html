About the tool

The fallhome-law-research-hub provides a secure way to manage your legal files. It runs as a browser-native application. This means you do not need to install complex server software. It stores your legal workflow data in a single file. You keep your data offline. This ensures your private information stays on your computer. Use this tool for US law research, inspection management, and tracking conflicts across your firm.

  • Web Browser: Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox.
  • Memory: At least 4GB of RAM.
  • Storage: 200MB of free space on your hard drive.

⚖️ Using the Application

The interface uses a simple layout for daily tasks. Open the application after you finish the setup. You will see a dashboard. This space shows your current research projects. To perform US law research, type your terms into the search bar. The tool filters through your offline database. It pulls relevant information for your inspection management needs.

Conflict tracking works through the firm-wide tab. Add client names to the tracking list. The software alerts you if a new case creates a potential conflict. Keep your data updated by clicking the save button. Because this app is a single-file system, saving creates a copy of your records in one location.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

Do you see an error message while running the software? First, check your internet connection. Some features require a bridge to your browser. You can restart the application if the window stops responding. Always back up your single data file to a separate drive or cloud storage. This protects your work against hardware failure.
