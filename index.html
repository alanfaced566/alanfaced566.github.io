Research Profile

Focus

Design, synthesis, characterization, and catalytic application of functional porous organic materials — with emphasis on Covalent Organic Frameworks (COFs).

Current Work

Ph.D. research on Functionalized Covalent Organic Frameworks for Energy Storage and Catalysis under Dr. Pradip Pachfule.

Electrocatalysis

Metal-embedded COFs for electrocatalytic applications, including oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) and highly stable vinylene-linked COFs.

Photocatalysis

Development of donor-functionalized and dimolybdenum paddlewheel-embedded COFs for photocatalytic H₂O₂ generation.
