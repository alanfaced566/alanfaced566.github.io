Research Profile

Focus

Design, synthesis, characterization, and catalytic application of functional porous organic materials with emphasis on Covalent Organic Frameworks (COFs).

Current Ph.D. Work

Functionalized Covalent Organic Frameworks for Energy Storage and Catalysis under Dr. Pradip Pachfule at SNBNCBS, Kolkata.

Electrocatalysis

Metal-embedded COFs for electrocatalytic applications including oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) and highly stable vinylene-linked COFs.

Photocatalysis

Donor-functionalized and dimolybdenum paddlewheel-embedded COFs for photocatalytic H₂O₂ generation and hydrogen evolution.
